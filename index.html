Call Flow Assistant

Interactive script for handling calls efficiently.

Step 1: Greeting & Verification
"Thanks for calling, my name is Rafat. Can you please confirm the account name?"
"Which language do you need assistance with today?"
Step 2: Call Setup & Info
"Do you need a third party dial-out or do you have the client with you?"
Step 3: Connect & Briefing
"Your LANGUAGE interpreter is on the line. Please hold while I bring them on the line."

To Interpreter:

"Thank you for answering. May I have your name and ID please?"
"I have a call from ACCOUNT requesting a LANGUAGE interpreter. The Call ID for your record is ____. Are you ready? Thank you, I am merging."
Step 4: Merge & Handover
"Thank you so much for holding. Your LANGUAGE interpreter is on the line. The interpreter's ID is ____, and for your record, the Call ID is ____."
"You mentioned no need to dial out, so I will let the interpreter proceed. Interpreter, please go ahead."
"Ma'am/Sir, you mentioned about dialing out to your client. May I have the number please? (Or just dial out)."
"Interpreter, please introduce yourself and gather any information you need before I place the call."
Reminder if needed:
"Interpreter, would you like to get the LANGUAGE speaker's name or leave a voicemail if they don't answer?"
Verification Pushbacks (If Client Refuses)

Use these phrases if the caller fails to provide required verification (Account Name/ID).

  • "So sorry, but the institution requires name verification first."
  • "I’d love to help, but I need to verify the account name."
  • "I am sorry, but account verification is needed to provide service."
  • "I apologize, strict guidelines from your facility require me to verify the name."
  • "Apologies, I cannot bypass the name verification step. I need a name match to move forward."
  • "Apologies, I cannot bypass the client ID verification step. I need a name/ID match to move forward."
