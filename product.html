Sharia Distribution Rules

• Wife: Receives 1/8 if there are children, otherwise 1/4 (shared among up to 4 wives).

• Husband: Receives 1/4 if there are children, otherwise 1/2.

• Parents: Each receive 1/6 if children exist. Father becomes Residuary if no sons exist.

• Daughter: 1/2 if only one, 2/3 if multiple (if no son). If sons exist, they share the residue in a 2:1 ratio.

• Aul/Radd: Automated adjustment of shares if the total exceeds or falls short of the whole property.
