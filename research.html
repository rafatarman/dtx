Master of Computer Science (Research)

My postgraduate research focuses on the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Artificial Intelligence within life-critical environments. I am specifically investigating how AI-driven predictive interfaces can optimize communication protocols in healthcare systems to reduce cognitive load and prevent fatal errors.

Phase I: Preliminary Survey on User Interaction with Critical Healthcare Interfaces

This research paper establishes the baseline data for my Masters' study. It utilizes a comprehensive preliminary survey to identify existing pain points in life-critical communication systems. The findings were used to develop a journal entry that highlights the urgent need for AI-integrated interaction models to safeguard patient data and system responses.

Analytical Report

The "Unseen Tax": Systemic Leakage in the Bangladeshi Context

A critical research report examining the fiscal environment of Bangladesh. This analysis explores the concept of "unseen taxes"—the financial burden placed on citizens through government money laundering, public fund mismanagement, and the "burning" of state assets. Using data-driven insights (facilitated by advanced AI models like Gemini), the report reveals how systemic corruption acts as a hidden economic drain on the general population.
