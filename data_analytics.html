05 // LIT REVIEW USER TRUST

HCI & User Trust

LITERATURE INSIGHT

Trust deficit is the primary barrier to digital health adoption in developing nations.

  • Transparent Security

    Visual cues of encryption and control reduce user anxiety.

  • Trust Transfer Theory

    Leveraging medical authority to validate digital tool credibility.

The Shield UI

Designing for "Visibility of Protection"

08 // CHALLENGES THE INTIMIDATION GAP

The Intimidation Gap

"Why is this so hard to use?"

Complex navigation hierarchies create a 'Wall of Fear' for non-expert users.

Steep Learning Curves

Current medical apps require high cognitive effort to navigate.

Cold User Experience

Clinical interfaces often lack empathy and human warmth.

Adaptation Burden

People are forced to adapt to technology, rather than vice versa.

13 // METHODOLOGY HCD FRAMEWORK

Human-Centered EHC Framework

Bridging "Trust" & "Time" Gaps

PHASE 01: ANALYSIS

(Listen & Learn)

Identify friction points & distrust sources.

  • Review Lit: EHR Usability Gaps
  • Context Inquiry: Golden Hour Workflow
  • User Research: Intimidation Surveys
ADDRESSES "TIME" & "TRUST" GAPS

PHASE 02: DESIGN

(Design & Prototype)

Develop high-fidelity UI/UX & visualize data.

  • Conceptualization: Wireframes: VitalCard
  • Visual Design: Age-friendly Cues
  • Prototyping: Interactive Sims
ADDRESSES THE "CLARITY GAP"

PHASE 03: EVALUATION

(Deploy & Test)

Validate effectiveness & confidence.

  • Usability Testing: Task-based Sessions
  • Data Collection: Errors & Time
  • Analysis: Compare Benchmarks
ADDRESSES THE "INTIMIDATION GAP"

FINAL DELIVERABLE: A validated UI/UX framework proven to save seconds & improve trust.

18 // INCLUSION EMPOWERING THE ELDERLY

Empowering the Elderly

DESIGN PILLAR

Digital equity is achieved only when the most vulnerable can navigate health systems without assistance.

  • Visual Aids

    High-contrast modes and oversized typography for low vision.

  • Assisted Navigation

    Simplified flows that account for motor-control decline.

Accessible by Default

Universal Design for Human Life
