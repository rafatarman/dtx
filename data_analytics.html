02 // INTRODUCTION CHAPTER 1: BACKGROUND

The Digital Health Shift

Integration of digital health is now a foundational pillar of Universal Health Coverage (UHC).

In Bangladesh, while NID systems reach 98% of the population, emergency health records remain analog and fragmented. Backend systems are technically robust but often fail due to a lack of Human-Centered Design (HCD).

The Adoption Void

Bridging the gap between secure systems and universal human accessibility.

03 // THE PROBLEM 4 CORE BOTTLENECKS

Systemic Gaps in Emergency Care

1. Time Crisis

Treatment delays in the "Golden Hour". Manual verification accounts for 48% of delays.

2. Trust Deficit

53.3% fear data surveillance; 43.3% worry about sensitive medical data misuse.

3. Intimidation Gap

Complex UIs designed for tech-savvy users exclude the elderly (59+) and low-literate.

4. Clarity Gap

Lack of visual hierarchy and glanceability for medics in high-pressure scenarios.

04 // LIT REVIEW CHAPTER 2: KEY THEORIES

Adoption & Trust Models

UTAUT2 Model

Focuses on Effort Expectancy: Ease of use is the primary predictor of uptake for elderly populations in developing nations.

Trust Transfer Theory

Utilizing visual UI anchors (encryption icons, certifications) to transfer trust from authorities to the digital tool.

Source: Norman (2013), Venkatesh et al. (2012), WHO Strategy on Digital Health (2020).
