03 // DEFINITIONTERMINOLOGY

Digital Emergency Health

Academic Definition:

The application of ICT to provide immediate medical assistance and care coordination during acute health crises.

World Health Organization. (2020). Global strategy on digital health 2020-2025. Geneva: WHO.

Why it Matters:

It bridges the "Administrative Void" in pre-hospital care. It focuses on Data Glanceability—ensuring a responder understands patient critical risks in under 5 seconds.

04 // CH 1BACKGROUND

The Global Digital Healthcare Shift

Universal Health Coverage (UHC) is now reliant on digital infrastructure. While Bangladesh has 98% NID penetration, emergency medical history remains fragmented and analog.

The Implementation Gap

Technical backend capability vs. Real-world human usability in crisis.

Systemic Bottlenecks & The 4 Core Gaps

05 // RPPROBLEM STATEMENT

The Socio-Technical Adoption Crisis

Time Crisis

Manual data checks account for 48% of delays.

Trust Deficit

53.3% fear surveillance misuse of data.

Intimidation

UI complexity creates tech-anxiety for seniors.

Clarity Gap

Records lack rapid glanceability for medics.

06 // MISSIONOBJECTIVES

Research Objectives (RO)

  • RO1

    To identify user needs and HCD requirements for emergency health cards in Bangladesh.

  • RO2

    To develop a validated Human-Centered UI/UX framework (DEHCMsync) bridging trust and intimidation gaps.

  • RO3

    To evaluate the impact of this framework on user satisfaction and clinical retrieval efficiency.

07 // INQUIRYQUESTIONS

Research Questions (RQ)

RQ1: Requirements

What specific HCD design requirements are essential to minimize data retrieval time in emergencies?

RQ2: Integration

How can accessibility principles and cultural trust markers be effectively integrated into a cohesive framework?

RQ3: Improvement

To what extent does the proposed framework improve usability metrics for non-tech-savvy users?
