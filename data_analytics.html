Chapter 1 // Background

The NID Binding Solution

Foundation

Bangladesh has 98% NID penetration, establishing a ready-made verification infrastructure for 170M+ citizens (Ahmed et al., 2026).

The Dataset Void

Despite NID, there is no synchronized digital health dataset. In emergencies, responders cannot recall patient history.

Binding Logic

By binding NID as the verification anchor, we can detect patients in seconds, track clinical data, and improvise treatment via history recall (Islam et al., 2024).

Chapter 1 // RP

The Socio-Technical Crisis

1. Time Crisis

48% avoidable delays due to manual verification protocols.

2. Trust Deficit

53.3% fear surveillance misuse of personal health data.

3. Intimidation Gap

Complex UIs exclude the elderly (59+) from digital schemes.

4. Clarity Gap

Unstructured data hinders medical glanceability during triage.
