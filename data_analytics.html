Chapter 1 // Background

The NID Binding Solution

Foundation

Bangladesh has 98% NID penetration, establishing a ready-made verification anchor (Ahmed et al., 2026).

Dataset Void

Despite NID, there is no synchronized digital health history for acute care scenarios.

Binding Logic

By binding NID as verification, responders can detect patients in seconds and improvise treatment based on history recall (Islam et al., 2024).
