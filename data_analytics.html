The Dataset Void

Despite NID coverage, there is no synchronized digital health history for acute clinical care scenarios.

Active Intelligence Logic

Binding NID as verification allows responders to detect patients in seconds and retrieve history instantly.

Critical Gaps

Socio-Technical Crisis

1. Time Crisis: Manual verification waste (5-10 mins) during clinical intake.

2. Trust Deficit: Fear of data exploitation; needs "Transparent Security" UI anchors.

3. Intimidation Gap: Navigation paralysis for seniors (59+) leads to exclusion.

4. Clarity Gap: Physicians struggle with unstructured data dumps during triage.
