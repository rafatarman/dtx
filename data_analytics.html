Chapter 1 // Background

The NID Binding Solution

98% Penetration

Bangladesh infrastructure provides a massive ready-made verification anchor (Ahmed et al., 2026).

The Dataset Void

Despite NID, there is no synchronized digital health history for acute care scenarios.

Active Intelligence Logic

By binding NID as verification, responders can detect patients in seconds and improvise treatment based on historical recall (Islam et al., 2024).

Chapter 1 // RP

The Socio-Technical Crisis

1. Time Crisis

48% avoidable delays due to manual verification protocols.

2. Trust Deficit

53.3% fear surveillance misuse of personal health data.

3. Intimidation Gap

Complex UIs exclude the elderly (59+) from digital schemes.

4. Clarity Gap

Unstructured data hinders medical glanceability during triage.
