03 // DEFINITION TERMINOLOGY

Digital Emergency Health

Academic Definition:

The application of ICT to provide immediate medical assistance and care coordination during acute health crises or accidents.

World Health Organization. (2020). Global strategy on digital health 2020-2025. Geneva: WHO. https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240010598

Why it Matters:

It shifts emergency medicine from reactive documentation to proactive data availability. It focuses on Glanceability—ensuring medical warnings are signaled in seconds during the critical Golden Hour.

05 // CH 1BACKGROUND

Global Shift vs. Analog Reality

Digital technologies are foundational for Universal Health Coverage (UHC). Bangladesh has 98% NID penetration, yet emergency care remains paper-reliant.

The Adoption Void

Technical backend capability vs. Real-world human usability in crisis.

06 // RPPROBLEM STATEMENT

The Socio-Technical Crisis

Time Crisis

Manual verification causes 48% of clinical delays.

Trust Deficit

53.3% fear surveillance misuse of data.

Intimidation

UI complexity creates technophobia for seniors.

Clarity Gap

Unstructured data hinders split-second triage.

13 // CH 3APPROACH

Defined Research Approach

ISO 9241-210 Standard

The study adopts a Non-Coding, Design-Centric approach focusing on the Interaction Layer. It utilizes iterative design cycles to center the paramedics' high-stress environment.

Research Type

Qualitative-dominant iterative framework with Quantitative validation via SUS (System Usability Scale) metrics.
