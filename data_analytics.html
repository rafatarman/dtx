05 // CH 1BACKGROUND

Global Paradigm vs. Local Reality

Systemic Foundation:

Digital technologies are foundational for Universal Health Coverage (UHC). Bangladesh has 98% NID penetration, yet emergency care remains paper-reliant.

Historical Blindness:

Absence of a digital health dataset means responders cannot recall any medical history, leading to lethal errors in acute care.

The Adoption Void

Sophisticated backend ecosystems fail when the "Human Interface" is ignored.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

06 // RPGAP ANALYSIS

1. Time Crisis (Emergency Gap)

Information blindness during the "Golden Hour" results in 48% of avoidable clinical delays.

2. Trust Deficit (Privacy Gap)

Deep surveillance anxieties prevent 53.3% of users from enrolling in digital health portals.

3. Intimidation Gap (Usability)

Complex, technocentric UIs create navigation paralysis for users aged 59+ and low-literate groups.

4. Clarity Gap (Visualization)

Unstructured data dumps lack medical glanceability, delaying split-second trauma triage.

13 // ANALYSISTECHNIQUES

Analytical Rigor

Qualitative: Thematic Coding

  • Transcription of interviews.
  • NVivo coding for cognitive friction mapping.
  • Identifying barriers to Trust and Intimidation.

Quantitative: Metrics

  • SUS: System Usability Scale (0-100 score).
  • Error Analysis: Frequency of mis-taps.
  • Time-on-Task: Paramedic retrieval speeds.

Triangulation of data ensures that design principles are statistically valid and humanly empathetic.
