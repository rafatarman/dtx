Chapter 01
INTRODUCTION
1.1 Problem Statement (RP)

Socio-Technical Gaps in Emergency Care

RP1: Time Crisis

First responders suffer from acute info blindness during the Golden Hour. 48% of delays are caused by manual paperwork processes.

RP2: Trust Deficit

Pervasive Trust Paradox exists where privacy fear blocks adoption. Users fear surveillance despite life-saving benefits.

RP3: Intimidation

Current interfaces prioritize technocentric complexity, creating navigation paralysis for users aged 59+.

1.2 Research Questions (RQ)

Targeted Inquiries

RQ1: What HCD requirements are essential to minimize data retrieval time for those with limited digital literacy?

RQ2: How can age-friendly interfaces and "Transparent Security" cues bridge intimidation and trust gaps?

RQ3: Does the DEHCMsync framework improve usability metrics, clinical efficiency, and user satisfaction?

1.3 Research Objectives (RO)

Strategic Roadmap

RO1: Identify

Identify specific needs and HCD requirements for the Bangladeshi context.

RO2: Develop

Develop a validated UI/UX framework (DEHCMsync) integrating trust cues.

RO3: Evaluate

Evaluate framework impact on satisfaction and retrieval speed benchmarks.

Future Research: These interactive UI assets are designed to be engineered into fully functional database-linked applications (Backend Implementation).
3.4 Evaluation Model

The SUS Method

System Usability Scale (SUS)

Instrument: 10-item Likert scale (1-5).

Calculation: Summing responses and applying the (x2.5) multiplier to generate a 0-100 score.

Benchmark: > 68 is Acceptable; > 80 is Excellence.

TAM Verification

Measuring Effort Expectancy (EE) and Performance Expectancy (PE) to predict adoption intention.
