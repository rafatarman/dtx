The NID Binding Solution

98% National Coverage

Bangladesh infrastructure provides a massive ready-made verification anchor.

(Ahmed et al., 2026)

The Dataset Void

Despite NID coverage, there is no synchronized digital history for acute clinical care.

Active Intelligence Logic

Binding NID allows responders to detect patients in seconds and retrieve life-critical recall data instantly.

The Gap

Socio-Technical Crisis

1. Time Crisis: Verification waste (5-10 mins) increases preventable mortality.
2. Trust Deficit: Fear of surveillance blocks adoption; needs "Transparent Security" UI anchors.
3. Intimidation Gap: Navigation paralysis for seniors (59+) leads to systematic health exclusion.
4. Clarity Gap: Physicians cannot find allergies in seconds among unstructured data dumps.

ISO 9241-210 Theory

Iterative Lifecycle

This study follows ISO standards to ensure sophisticated tech does not compromise usability. Focus is strictly on the Context of Use: stressed paramedics and cognitive decline in seniors.

Interaction Cues

  • Age-Friendly: High-contrast visuals.
  • Glanceability: Medical iconography.
  • Equity: Reduced cognitive load.

UTAUT2 & Trust Transfer

Effort Expectancy

Using UTAUT2 to minimize effort required by elderly users, reducing tech-anxiety triggers.

Trust Transfer Theory

Facilitating transfer of trust from hospitals to digital artifacts via "Transparent Security" markers.

Digital Inequality

S. Asian Gap

Technical Efficacy

HCD Lifecycle Design

1. Discovery

Friction mapping and interviews (N=20) to identify lethal triage delays.

2. Prototype

High-fidelity Figma simulations with Trust-Transfer cues.

3. Validation

Thinking Aloud sessions, SUS metrics, and expert clinical review.

Triangulation Rigor

Qualitative

Thematic coding via NVivo to map interaction barriers and technophobia triggers.

Quantitative

SUS Likert analysis and paramedic retrieval speed benchmarks (Target: < 30s).
