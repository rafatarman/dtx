Field Observation: The Mechanics of Theft

"In my research, I have observed that data theft is rarely a 'loud' event. It happens through 'Information Seating'—where attackers plant themselves within social media trust loops, observing habits before ever executing a line of code. I found that people often hand over their entire digital identity through seemingly harmless social media interactions, where metadata scraping and profile linking do the work of a thousand hackers."
